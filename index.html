Where it sits, and why that matters

  • Behind MFM Prayer City — on the Lagos–Ibadan Expressway axis in Ogun State.
  • ~10 minutes from the Lagos–Ibadan Expressway to the estate gate.
  • ~15 minutes from Ojodu Berger, the nearest major Lagos bus-stop.
  • Close to established landmarks: Mountain Top University, Punch Newspaper, Magboro Mall, Justrite Supermarket and Bulwark International School.

Magboro sits right on Lagos's doorstep in Obafemi-Owode LGA, Ogun State — one of a handful of expressway towns (alongside Mowe and Ibafo) that have grown fastest over the last decade as Lagos workers look for land and housing they can actually afford within commuting distance of Ojodu Berger, Berger and the rest of mainland Lagos. That same growth is what has taken land prices in this estate from what earlier buyers paid to the ₦7.5M the developer now charges — this plot is offered below that, directly by the owner.
